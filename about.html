ABOUT US

TO LEARN TO LEAD

Our Profile & History

Good Shepherd Higher Secondary School is managed by Good Shepherd Church Chapahati. Our institution stands as a structural symbol of quality education, deep-rooted ethical values, and holistic human growth.

We are wholeheartedly committed to building equitable learning systems. Our systems support all diverse student populations—including Garos, Hajongs, Bengalis, and others—irrespective of local caste, background, or economic status. The school has conducted various activities aimed at promoting holistic development and strengthening community ties through education.

Educational & Co-Curricular Programs

We conduct structured regular academic classes from 9:00 am to 2:35 pm. To evaluate learning standards, we organize one Unit Test before the Half Yearly Examinations and an additional Unit Test before the final Annual Examinations. Notably, during the last three consecutive years, our institution has proudly secured 100% passing metrics in the official SSLC board examinations.

Parent-Teacher Meetings (PTMs)

Regular interactive meetings map individual developmental progress and track campus initiatives. These metrics maximize foundational home environments.

Frequency: Quarterly

Teachers' Day Celebrations

Observed on September 5 annually to honor dedicated instructors. Features skits, student speeches, and symbolic performance acts.

Timeline: Sept 5th

Children’s Day Programs

Dedicated celebrations designed around recreational balance, student-centric focus projects, and community values.

Timeline: Annual

Annual & Cultural Functions

Annual Main Function

Brings together parents, village elders, and local dignitaries to acknowledge student honors, distribute academic excellence certificates, and build strong civic connections.

Cultural Showcase Programs

Provides dedicated structural platforms honoring the authentic cultural layout of Garos, Hajongs, and Bengalis through dance styles, music arts, and historical skits.

Physical Infrastructure & Social Responsibility

Games & Sports Meet

Annual sports days host track races, complex team assignments, and individual field athletics to instill systematic teamwork, daily physical health, and self-discipline parameters.

Underprivileged Education Outreach

Central to the core mission values of the Good Shepherd Church context, this continuous structure gives targeted educational materials and access to local village students regardless of class constraints.

Conclusion

Good Shepherd Higher Secondary School continues to provide a well-rounded educational experience, promoting intellectual, cultural, and physical development. Through events and social outreach, the school remains committed to being a beacon of hope for the community and fostering a spirit of unity and learning among students.
