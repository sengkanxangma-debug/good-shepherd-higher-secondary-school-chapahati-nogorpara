ABOUT US

TO LEARN TO LEAD

Our Profile & History

Good Shepherd Higher Secondary School is managed by Good Shepherd Church Chapahati. Our institution stands as a structural symbol of quality education, deep-rooted ethical values, and holistic human growth.

We are wholeheartedly committed to building equitable learning systems. Our systems support all diverse student populations—including Garos, Hajongs, Bengalis, and others—irrespective of local caste, background, or economic status. The school has conducted various activities aimed at promoting holistic development and strengthening community ties through education.

Educational & Co-Curricular Programs

We conduct structured regular academic classes from 9:00 am to 2:35 pm. To evaluate learning standards, we organize one Unit Test before the Half Yearly Examinations and an additional Unit Test before the final Annual Examinations. Notably, during the last three consecutive years, our institution has proudly secured 100% passing metrics in the official SSLC board examinations.

Parent-Teacher Meetings (PTMs)

Regular interactive meetings map individual developmental progress and track campus initiatives. These metrics maximize foundational home environments.

Frequency: Quarterly

Teachers' Day Celebrations

Observed on September 5 annually to honor dedicated instructors. Features skits, student speeches, and symbolic performance acts.

Timeline: Sept 5th

Children’s Day Programs

Dedicated celebrations designed around recreational balance, student-centric focus projects, and community values.

Timeline: Annual

Annual & Cultural Functions

Annual Main Function

Brings together parents, village elders, and local dignitaries to acknowledge student honors, distribute academic excellence certificates, and build strong civic connections.

Cultural Showcase Programs

Provides dedicated structural platforms honoring the authentic cultural layout of Garos, Hajongs, and Bengalis through dance styles, music arts, and historical skits.

Physical Infrastructure & Social Responsibility

Games & Sports Meet

Annual sports days host track races, complex team assignments, and individual field athletics to instill systematic teamwork, daily physical health, and self-discipline parameters.

Underprivileged Education Outreach

Central to the core mission values of the Good Shepherd Church context, this continuous structure gives targeted educational materials and access to local village students regardless of class constraints.

Conclusion

Good Shepherd Higher Secondary School continues to provide a well-rounded educational experience, promoting intellectual, cultural, and physical development. Through events and social outreach, the school remains committed to being a beacon of hope for the community and fostering a spirit of unity and learning among students.

School Policies & Procedures

Learn about our admission process, fee structure, and the essential protocols designed to maintain an organized and effective educational environment.

Admission and Withdrawal

  1. New admissions are made only to Nursery Class.[cite: 6] Other admission, if any, will depend on the availability of seats and on the competence of the student.[cite: 6]
  2. For new admission to Nursery the minimum age should be 4 and the maximum should be 6.[cite: 6]
  3. New students must be introduced personally by their parents/guardians who will be responsible for their regularity, conduct and fees.[cite: 6]
  4. A student who has attended a recognised school can be admitted only on the strength of the Transfer Certificate, Character Certificate from the Head of the school last attended and the Original Mark-sheet of the examination last appeared.[cite: 6] Admissions are made only after an admission test.[cite: 6] Satisfactory performance is a must for admission.[cite: 6]
  5. Only parents/guardians may apply for Transfer Certificate which must be made in writing.[cite: 6]
  6. No students whose fees are in arrears be given a Transfer Certificate.[cite: 6]
  7. A fees of Rs. 100/- will be charged whenever an application for Transfer Certificate or a duplicate of the same is made.[cite: 6]
  8. A student failing two years in succession in the same class is to be discontinued his / her studies in he school.[cite: 6]

Fees

  1. All fees except tuition fees and contingency fees are to be paid at the time of admission.[cite: 8] Tuition fees can be paid without fine, till the 15th of every month.[cite: 8] For delayed payments, a fine of Rs. 10 per month will be imposed.[cite: 8]
  2. The school fees cover 12 months.[cite: 8] No deduction is made for holidays or broken periods.[cite: 8] Students are liable to pay as long as their names are on the rolls.[cite: 8]
  3. All fees and dues are to be cleared before the examinations both Half Yearly and Final.[cite: 8]
  4. School fees are liable to be revised at any time.[cite: 8]

Rules and Regulations

Discipline, punctuality, and moral integrity are the core pillars of our institution. Review the general rules that every student must follow.

General Rules of the School

  1. The school hours begin at 9:00 a.m. end at 2:35 p.m.[cite: 5] For Nursery and K.G. they are from 9:00 a.m. to 12:00 p.m.[cite: 5] All students are to be in the school compound by 8:45 a.m. for the Assembly.[cite: 5]
  2. Every student must posses a School Diary which must be brought to school daily.[cite: 5]
  3. For irregular attendance, habitual negligence in study, disobedience, rebelliousness or any conduct injurious to the moral tone of the school, a student is liable to be dismissed or otherwise seriously dealt with by the school authority.[cite: 5]
  4. Mobile phones, Electronic devices, Sharp objects, Books & Magazines other than Text books and other materials of objectionable nature shall not be brought to the school premises.[cite: 5] If found they will be confiscated and will not be given back.[cite: 5]
  5. Students are requested to use their home address for all mail directed to them.[cite: 5] The Principal reserves the right to open the mail of students who use school address.[cite: 5]
  6. Scribling on the wall or furniture or floor is to be strictly avoided.[cite: 5] Any damage to the school property including games and sports equipment will have to be made good by the student concerned.[cite: 5] The decision of the Principal/Headmistress regarding the compensation shall be final.[cite: 5]
  7. Students are responsible for the safe custody of their belongings.[cite: 5] The school assumes no responsibility for them.[cite: 5]
  8. Smoking, chewing of betel-nut or tobacco, drinking alcohol and the use of drugs in any form by students in the school as well as at home are strictly prohibited.[cite: 5] Those who indulge in such activities will be dismissed from the school immediately.[cite: 5]
  9. Parents / Guardians are not allowed neither to see their children or interview teachers during the school hours nor they are allowed to be present in the class room or around while classes are in progress.[cite: 5]
  10. During the recess, no student will be allowed to leave the school premises without the written permission of the Principal.[cite: 5]
  11. Any recommendation, request or complain made by the parents / guardians should be done in written and to be addressed to the principal and not to the Class Teacher or any staff member.[cite: 5]
  12. Every student attending the school is obliged to take part in all the co-curricular activities of the school as part of their civic training and no one will be exempted unless for valid reasons.[cite: 5]
  13. No private tuition for regular students will be allowed in the school campus.[cite: 5]
  14. Students are forbidden to raise collections in the school or outside without the written permission of the Principal.[cite: 5]
  15. This school being a registered institution under the Catholic Schools Education Board, Diocese of Tura, all matters relating to School Curriculum, Text Books, Examinations, Vacations etc. are subjected to the decisions of the said board.[cite: 5] Suggestions and complaints, if any, are to be addressed to the board through the Principal of the institution.[cite: 5]
  16. Stealing and forging of signatures are serious faults and anybody found guilty is liable to be dismissed immediately.[cite: 5]
  17. The school cannot be held responsible accidents small or serious, to pupil during their stay in the school or while taking part in sports and other co-curricular activities within or outside the school premises.[cite: 5]
  18. Parents/guardians are expected to make proper arrangements to collect their children immediately after the classes.[cite: 6] Though proper care will be taken to ensure the safety of the children, the school cannot be held responsible if a student's leaves the school campus on his / her own accord.[cite: 6]

General Rules of Discipline

  1. Obedience, regularity and punctuality will be strictly maintained and exacted in the school.[cite: 6]
  2. Discipline and honesty in words and deeds will be demanded and inculcated in the school.[cite: 6]
  3. Moral instructions will be compulsory in the school.[cite: 6] No student will be allowed to stay away during such instructions.[cite: 6]
  4. Politeness and courtesy of speech and conduct as well as cleanliness of dress and person will be inculcated and demanded in the school.[cite: 6]
  5. No child suffering from a contagious disease shall be permitted to attend the School.[cite: 6]
  6. Parents /Guardians cannot dictate to the authorities as they alone have the right to say on what condition they will admit or retain a student in the institution.[cite: 6]
  7. No act of indiscipline, interference in the administration disrespect to any teacher or to any religion or community by any student or their parents shall be tolerated.[cite: 6] No activities other than academic or related to the institution will be permitted in the school campus.[cite: 6]
  8. In case of disputes of any nature, the decision of the school authority which consists of the Principal and the Managing Committee shall be final.[cite: 6]
  9. The school rules and regulations can be modified or additions can be made, if so warranted, without prior notice.[cite: 6]

Academics & Attendance

Regular attendance and honest academic practices are non-negotiable. Explore our leave protocols and examination structure.

Absence and Leave

  1. No student should absent himself / herself from the school without obtaining leave previously.[cite: 6]
  2. Staying away from class, leaving the school without permission is a serious breach of discipline.[cite: 6] If a student has to go home during the school hours, there must be a written application from his / her parent / guardian stating the reason, which must be serious one.[cite: 7] In all the cases the Principal's permission is required.[cite: 7]
  3. The reason for leave or absence must be entered by the parent / guardian in the Absence and Leave Record column of the School Diary.[cite: 7] Reasons of a private nature may be submitted by a letter.[cite: 7] Birthdays, picnics, urgent business, rain etc. will not be considered sufficient reasons for absence nor a student is allowed to take a leave for studies.[cite: 7] Forging signatures in this regard is considered a serious fault and if any one found doing so is liable to be punished accordingly.[cite: 7]
  4. A student who has been absent from class may attend class after his /her absence has been condoned by the Principal / Headmistress in the Absence Record column.[cite: 7] No late comer may enter his/her class without the permission of the Principal.[cite: 7] Students who are habitually late are liable to be punished or fined.[cite: 7]
  5. A student who absences himself/herself from class for one calender month or more without the prior information will have his/her name struck of from the register.[cite: 7] In case of prolonged sickness a Medical Certificate is to be produced.[cite: 7] A student with less than 75% attendance shall not be promoted.[cite: 7]
  6. Students must rejoin classes after the holidays on the re-opening day.[cite: 7] Any breach of this rule will be considered a serious offence.[cite: 7] A fine of Rs. 100/- per day will be imposed on violation of thie rule.[cite: 7]
  7. The school accepts no responsibility if a pupil is obliged to return home during the class hours for failure to wear the school uniform or to bring books to the class or to produce the signature of parents on remarks written by the teachers in the attendance record of the calender.[cite: 7]

Examination and Promotion

  1. For student to pass, 40% marks should be obtained for all compulsory subjects.[cite: 7]
  2. 30% of the Annual Examination marks are reserved for the various tests and examination held during the year.[cite: 7]
  3. Failure to appear in any test or examination, and irregular attendance can seriously affect a student's final results and promotion.[cite: 7]
  4. No re-examination will be permitted for absence during examination.[cite: 7] In case of illness a Medical Certificate is to be produced.[cite: 7]
  5. No students whose fees are in arrears will be admitted for examination both Half Yearly and Final.[cite: 7]
  6. Dishonest practices like copying is considered a serious violation of examination rules.[cite: 7] A student indulges in such practices, if caught, will not be allowed to write the examination and his / her result will be withheld.[cite: 7] He / She can be expelled from the school if the authority so wishes.[cite: 7]
  7. A student who fails in three or more subjects may be considered to have failed.[cite: 7]
  8. Marks and remarks of examinations and the students progress will be given in the Progress Report.[cite: 7] Parents / Guardians should examine and sign it regularly and follow up the progress of their ward.[cite: 7] A student found with the Progress Report unsigned will not be admitted in the class.[cite: 7] Forging signature, alternating of Marks or remarks given in Progress Report is a serious violation of examination rules.[cite: 7] Any one found doing so will be punished accordingly.[cite: 7]
  9. Promotion is decided at the staff meeting after considering all aspects of a student's progress throughout the year, including his / her behaviour and conduct.[cite: 8] Hence results are final and cannot be re-considered.[cite: 8] Final Examination answer scripts shall not be shown to the student.[cite: 8]

Parental Co-operation

The school does not guarantee a student's success without parental co-operation.[cite: 9] They are to co-operate with the school authority by :[cite: 9]

  • Seeing that their ward is punctual and regular in attending class and other activities of the school.[cite: 9]
  • Seeing that their children do their home work and prepare their lessons daily.[cite: 9]
  • Following up the student's progress by checking the Progress Report and notifications in the School Diary.[cite: 9]
  • Occasionally meeting the school authorities to discuss with them means of bringing about greater progress in their ward's education both scholastic and moral.[cite: 9]
  • Insisting cleanliness and neatness in dress and appearance.[cite: 9]
  • By co-operating with the school authorities in implementing school rules and regulations and discipline.[cite: 9]
