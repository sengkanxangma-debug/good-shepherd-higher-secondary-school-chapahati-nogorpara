School History

Good Shepherd Higher Secondary School, Chapahati - Nogorpara is a premier educational institution managed by the Good Shepherd Church, Chapahati, in South West Garo Hills, Meghalaya, and is affiliated to the Meghalaya Board of School Education (MBOSE)_

Founded with a noble mission to bring quality, value-based English-medium education to the children of the region, the school began as a humble centre of learning and steadily grew through decades of dedicated service and community trust.

Extending from the Pre-Primary level up to the Higher Secondary Arts Stream (Pioneer Batch 2026–2027), the institution has moulded generations of disciplined scholars, leaders, and responsible citizens across diverse backgrounds.

Official School Emblem & Guiding Motto:
"To Learn, To Lead"

Our sacred emblem and guiding motto, "To Learn, To Lead," embody the spiritual, moral, and academic vision of Good Shepherd Higher Secondary School. The centerpiece of our crest draws inspiration from the Good Shepherd, symbolizing compassionate guidance, protection, and selfless care for every learner_

Meaning of the School Logo:
• The Good Shepherd & Cross: Represents faith, moral integrity, and dedicated mentorship where teachers nurture every child with patience and love.
• The Open Book & Light of Knowledge: Signifies the pursuit of truth, intellectual enlightenment, and dispelling ignorance through holistic education.
• The Shield & Crest: Stands for resilience, character formation, unity, and striving for excellence in both academics and co-curricular life.

Meaning of the School Motto ("To Learn, To Lead"):
True leadership begins with humble learning. "To Learn" calls every student to acquire knowledge, discipline, and moral virtue; "To Lead" empowers them to step into society as compassionate, ethical trailblazers who uplift their community and nation.

Principal's Message

Dear Friends,

Welcome to Good Shepherd Higher Secondary School, Chapahati Nogorpara! I gladly welcome all of you to our enriching website, which I am sure will serve as an essential interactive tool for all of you to acquaint yourselves with our large and extended school family.

Over decades of relentless academic endeavour and brilliance, our school has become a much sought-after educational institution that has successfully moulded generations of students hailing from diverse ethnic, religious, linguistic, and social backgrounds. It has produced and continues to produce eminent personalities in myriad walks of life_

Our school has initiated a series of progressive steps to keep pace with the changing times to meet the needs of our students. We take immense pride in our modern classroom learning solutions and student-centered learning processes, where teachers act as true facilitators. Our guiding principle remains the integral development of students with a holistic approach, where virtue and knowledge are what we passionately strive for. The net result is that in both academic and co-curricular realms, our students are second to none.

Looking forward to your frequent visits to our website.
